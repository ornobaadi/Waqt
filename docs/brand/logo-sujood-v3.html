Waqt — app icon concepts, sujood, round 3

1 · Light upon the Mat
Light from above falling on a believer in sujood on the prayer mat.
2 · Gold on Teal
The same moment in gold on deep teal: warm and distinct on a home screen.
3 · Under the Night Sky
Sujood beneath the crescent: the last third of the night, when the servant is closest.
4 · Night and Light
Stars, crescent and a gentle beam over a believer in sujood: calm and clear.
5 · Sujood at Dawn
A believer in sujood as the sun rises: the day begins on the prayer mat.
