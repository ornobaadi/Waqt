Waqt — app icon concepts, round 4

1 · Hourglass
Time running through the glass, golden and precious: every grain is a chance to draw near to Allah.
2 · Astrolabe
The instrument Muslim scholars built to find the prayer times: time, the Islamic way.
3 · Steps to the Light
Small steps up toward a lit mihrab: getting closer to Allah, one sunnah at a time.
4 · Five across the Sky
The five prayers placed along the sun's path: the whole day measured by salah.
5 · Closest in Sujood
"The closest a servant is to his Lord is in sujood" (Muslim): light falling on a believer in prostration.
6 · Tasbih Clock
Thirty-three beads become the face of a clock: dhikr that fills the hours.
7 · Drawing Near
Arches within arches, brighter toward the centre: each step inward brings you closer to Allah.
8 · Mihrab Clock
A clock set inside a mihrab: every hour turned toward Allah.
9 · From Night to Dawn
The crescent of the night and the sun at the horizon: every waqt, from Maghrib to Fajr and back.
10 · The Last Third
The night in three parts, the last one lit: when Allah draws near and asks who is calling on Him.
