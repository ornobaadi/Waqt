Waqt — logo concepts, round 2

1 · Crescent & Star of Time
The crescent that begins every Hijri month, with an eight-point star: time, the Islamic way.
2 · Mosque at Sunrise
A mosque against the rising sun: a Muslim's day is built around the prayer.
3 · Hands of Dua
Two hands raised in dua beneath the crescent: the dua for this moment is the heart of the app.
4 · Ramadan Lantern
A fanous glowing with a crescent: light for the special nights and days of the year.
5 · Mihrab from Night to Dawn
Inside a mihrab the sky turns from crescent night to sunrise: every moment of the day in one frame.
6 · Tasbih & Crescent
The 33-bead tasbih around a crescent: dhikr through every day of the month.
7 · Prayer Mat
The sajjadah with its mihrab: the place you return to five times a day.
8 · Rub el Hizb Crescent
The eight-pointed star from the Mushaf margins holding a crescent: Qur'an, sunnah and time together.
9 · وقت Calligraphy
The word Waqt ("time") in Arabic under a small crescent. System font for now; the final would be hand-drawn.
10 · Book on the Rehal
An open book on its stand under the crescent: learning how the Prophet ﷺ lived each day.
