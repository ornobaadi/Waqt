Waqt — app icon concepts, round 3

1 · Dome under the Day
A masjid dome beneath the sun's path: the prayer anchors every moment of the day.
2 · Dome Clock
The dome carries the hands of a clock: prayer and time, one shape.
3 · Crescent Cradle
A true crescent holding a small dome: the Hijri month and the masjid together.
4 · Mihrab Moment
A mihrab framing the night crescent and the coming dawn: the right moment for worship.
5 · Masjid at Sunrise
Dome, minarets and the rising sun: the day of a Muslim starts with the prayer.
6 · Ramadan Lantern
A fanous glowing with a crescent: light for Ramadan and the special nights.
7 · Crescent Clock
A clock face resting in the crescent: the Islamic hours of the day and night.
8 · Star & Dome
The eight-pointed star of Islamic art crowning a dome: tradition, made clean.
9 · Book & Dome
A dome rising from an open book: learning the sunnah of each day and prayer.
10 · The Blessed Night
Dome, minaret, crescent and stars: the nights you are waiting for, and the duas for them.
