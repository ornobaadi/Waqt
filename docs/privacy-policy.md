# Waqt privacy policy

_Last updated: 6 November 2026_

Waqt is built to work entirely on your phone. Here is exactly what that means.

## What Waqt collects
**Nothing.** Waqt has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. It does not send data to us or to anyone else.

## Location
- When you choose **Use my location**, Waqt asks Android for a city-level (coarse) position, once.
- That position is used on the phone to calculate prayer times and to name your nearest city from a list stored inside the app.
- It is saved only on your phone, so prayer times keep working offline. It is never transmitted.
- You can choose a city by hand instead and never grant location permission.

## Notifications and alarms
Reminders are scheduled on the phone with Android's alarm system. Nothing about them leaves the device.

## Settings and checklists
Your settings, reminder choices and checklist ticks are stored on the phone. Uninstalling Waqt deletes them.

## Links you open
Waqt can open a hadith or Qur'an reference on sunnah.com, quran.com or dorar.net in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Problem reports
Waqt keeps a short log of app errors on the phone. **Report a problem** and **Report an issue with this text** open a pre-filled email draft in your mail app. You see the full text before anything is sent, and nothing is sent unless you submit it. Reports contain the app version and, for problem reports, recent error messages. They never contain your location.

## Children
Waqt is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
