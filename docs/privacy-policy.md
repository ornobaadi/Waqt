# Waqt privacy policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Waqt is built to work entirely on your phone. Here is exactly what that means.

## What Waqt collects
**Nothing.** Waqt has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service. It does not send data about you to us or to anyone else.

## Location
- When you choose **Use my location**, Waqt asks Android for a city-level (coarse) position, once.
- That position is used on the phone to calculate prayer times and to name your nearest city from a list stored inside the app.
- It is saved only on your phone, so prayer times keep working offline. It is never transmitted.
- You can choose a city by hand instead and never grant location permission.

## Qibla
The Qibla finder reads the phone's compass and motion sensors on the phone, only while the Qibla screen is open. The direction is worked out from your chosen city. Sensor readings are never stored or sent.

## Islamic calendar
Hijri months begin when the crescent is sighted, so Waqt downloads the announced start dates of the months around today from api.aladhan.com, for the authority you choose in Settings (Saudi High Judiciary Council or Diyanet). The request contains only a Hijri month, a year and the authority's name: no location, no identifier, nothing about you. Choose **Local moon sighting** and Waqt makes no network request at all.

## Hadith library (downloaded once, if you ask)
The hadith library is not inside the app. When you first search the hadith, Waqt offers to download it in your language (a few MB) from hadeethenc.com. It is a plain file download: nothing about you is sent. After that it stays on your phone and works offline.

## Quran translation and recitation (if you ask)
The Arabic text of the Qur'an is inside the app. The translation for your language is downloaded once, when you ask, from quranenc.com; Waqt also asks quranenc.com for that translation's version number. Recitations are played from everyayah.com, either streamed while you listen or downloaded surah by surah when you choose. These are plain file requests: they contain the file's name and nothing about you. Waqt remembers where you stopped reading and the ayat you saved on your phone only. Unless you allow mobile data in the reading settings, streaming and downloads wait for Wi-Fi, which is why Waqt checks whether the phone is on Wi-Fi or mobile data.

## Notifications and alarms
Reminders are scheduled on the phone with Android's alarm system. Nothing about them leaves the device.

## Settings and checklists
Your settings, reminder choices and checklist ticks are stored on the phone. Uninstalling Waqt deletes them.

## Links you open
Waqt can open a hadith or Qur'an reference on sunnah.com, quran.com or dorar.net in your browser. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

## Problem reports
Waqt keeps a short log of app errors on the phone. **Report a problem** and **Report an issue with this text** open a pre-filled email draft in your mail app. You see the full text before anything is sent, and nothing is sent unless you submit it. Reports contain the app version and, for problem reports, recent error messages. They never contain your location.

## Children
Waqt is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date.
